More Hiking, Less Driving

This guide identifies nature hiking opportunities within a 20-minute driving time of the Town of Franklin. Proximity is based on travel time rather than straight-line measurement to account for mountain terrain and access points. Whether you are looking for a short walk or a more adventurous hike, you can explore and experience nature close to home.

Trail Categories & Details

Bartram Trail

  • Bessie’s Loop: 6 min drive, 1.1 mi loop, 250 ft elev. Forest road, Ray Cove Falls, creek crossings.
  • William’s Pulpit: 6 min, 4.2 mi, 1080 ft. Climb ridge to rock overlook with broad mountain views.
  • Hickory Knoll Rd. to the Pinnacle: 9 min, 4.5 mi, 1340 ft. Uphill through forest, mountain vista at summit.

Old Road Bed

  • Mud Creek: 19 min, 2.3 mi, 240 ft. Passes old railway, follows creek to Mud Creek Falls (check private property signage).

Mainspring

  • Queen’s Branch: 15 min, 0.6 mi, 50 ft. Old US-28 bed along Little Tennessee River. Swimming possible.
  • Gibson Bottoms: 6 min, 1.7 mi, 40/170 ft. Riverside woods/floodplain trail.
  • Onion Mountain North: 14 min, 2-5 mi, 490/1.6k ft. Ridge access, dual-ridge views.
  • Onion Mountain South: 12 min, 2-5 mi, 600/1.6k ft. Begins Keller’s Climb; woods & pastures.
  • Tessentee Bottomland: 11 min, 1-3 mi, 120 ft. Bottomland woods, streams, old farm bldgs.

Appalachian Trail

  • Winding Stair Gap to Siler Bald: 12 min, 9 mi, 1790 ft. Uphill climb with outstanding views from the Bald.
  • Winding Stair Gap South: 12 min, varies. Northern terminus, creek crossings, wildlife.

Park

  • Parker Meadows: 6 min, 1.1 mi, 250 ft. Community park, partially shaded walking loop by creek.

Greenway

  • Suli Marsh: 1 min, 1.5 mi, 190 ft. Swamp trail/boardwalk, old metal bridge. US-28 access.
  • Big Bear Park: Playground, dog park, beaver dam area walk.
  • Riverwalk: Behind Shops at Riverwalk—shelter, garden, 0.25mi exercise loop.
  • Salali Ln: River picnic point, soccer fields, swings.
  • Tassee Park: Pavilion, boat access, geocache spot.
  • Southwestern Community College: College trail to greenway & garden.
  • Library: Short access down to greenway and bottomland loop at Siler Farm Rd.

Schools (after school hours)

  • Cartoogechaye Loop: 5 min, 0.25 mi, 0 ft. Paved loop, shaded pavilion.
  • Cove Branch (MVI): 4 min, 0.3 mi, 0 ft. School nature/restoration project.
  • Macon Middle Loop: 4 min, 0.5 mi, 20 ft. Nature trail, flying kites.
  • South Macon Loop: 4 min, 0.5 mi, 20 ft. Paved spur to creek overlook—look for birds/amphibians.
