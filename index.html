Trail Guide

Nearcountry Trails of Franklin, NC

This guide identifies nature hiking opportunities within a 20-minute driving time of the Town of Franklin. Proximity is based on travel time rather than straight-line measurement to better account for mountain terrain and indirect access points when identifying nearby hikes. Whether you are looking for a short walk or a more adventurous hike, you can explore and experience nature close to home.

Hike Legend

  • Travel Time: Estimated driving time from Franklin.
  • Hike Length: Total distance in miles.
  • Elevation Change: Total feet of elevation change.
  • Route Types: Loop, Out and Back, or Trail Network.

Bartram Trail

1) Bessie’s Loop

  • Travel Time: 6 minutes
  • Hike Length: 1.1 miles
  • Elevation Change: 250 feet
  • Route Type: Loop

Start at the Wallace Branch Bartram Trail entrance at the end of Ray Cove Rd. This loop follows the trail to a Forest Service Road, then turns left to return toward the parking area. Along the way, you'll pass Ray Cove Falls and make several creek crossings.

2) William’s Pulpit

  • Travel Time: 6 minutes
  • Hike Length: 4.2 miles
  • Elevation Change: 1,080 feet
  • Route Type: Out and Back

Start at the Wallace Branch Bartram Trail entrance at the end of Ray Cove Rd. The trail climbs to the ridgeline and continues to William's Pulpit, a steep rock outcropping with long-range mountain views, better in winter and early spring.

3) Hickory Knoll Rd. to The Pinnacle

  • Travel Time: 9 minutes
  • Hike Length: 4.5 miles
  • Elevation Change: 1,340 feet
  • Route Type: Out and Back

Start at Hickory Knoll Rd on the Bartram Trail for a steady uphill hike through Nantahala National Forest. The route passes rocky, wooded terrain and ends at The Pinnacle, where a broad mountain view rewards the climb.

Old Road Bed

4) Mud Creek

  • Travel Time: 19 minutes
  • Hike Length: 2.3 miles
  • Elevation Change: 240 feet
  • Route Type: Out and Back

After admiring the view at Sky Valley Overlook, walk east behind the guard rail, passing the remnants of a bridge above Estatoah Falls before reaching an old road bed that leads into the woods. Follow the easy creekside walk to the cascading 100-foot Mud Creek Falls. Be mindful of private property.

Mainspring

5) Queen’s Branch

  • Travel Time: 15 minutes
  • Hike Length: 0.6 miles
  • Elevation Change: 50 feet
  • Route Type: Out and Back

A beautiful trail off US-28 along the Little Tennessee River, popular for swimming.

6) Gibson Bottoms

  • Travel Time: 6 minutes
  • Hike Length: 1.7 miles
  • Elevation Change: 40-170 feet
  • Route Type: Trail Network

Just off Sanderstown, down Rain Ridge Rd, this trail network hugs the Little Tennessee River, passes by cow pastures, and winds through wooded slopes.

7) Onion Mountain North

  • Travel Time: 14 minutes
  • Hike Length: 2-5 miles
  • Elevation Change: 490-1.6k feet
  • Route Type: Trail Network

This entrance offers access to the higher northern side of the preserve by way of Onion Mtn. Rd. Don't miss the views from both sides of the ridge as you approach the peak by way of Jordan's Journey.

8) Onion Mountain South

  • Travel Time: 12 minutes
  • Hike Length: 2-5 miles
  • Elevation Change: 600-1.6k feet
  • Route Type: Trail Network

Access from Elijay Rd begins with Kelder's Climb, which starts at a comfortable grade before rising steeply to the property's peak. A gentler spur, Sarah's Sojourn, turns around at a modest waterfall.

9) Tessentee Bottomland

  • Travel Time: 11 minutes
  • Hike Length: 1-3 miles
  • Elevation Change: 120 feet
  • Route Type: Trail Network

Just south of the Bartram Trail on Hickory Knoll Rd, this trail network winds through bottomland forest, creekside pastures, and 100-year old farm buildings.

Appalachian Trail

10) Winding Stair Gap to Siler Bald

  • Travel Time: 12 minutes
  • Hike Length: 9 miles
  • Elevation Change: 1,790 feet
  • Route Type: Out and Back

This trailhead doubles the hiking time to Siler Bald, but cuts the driving time in half. If you are looking for an uphill hike with spectacular views, this is it.

11) Winding Stair Gap South

  • Travel Time: 12 minutes
  • Hike Length: Varies
  • Elevation Change: Varies
  • Route Type: Out and Back

Less majestic than its northern counterpart, this trail still offers wildflowers and some creek crossings.

Park

12) Parker Meadows

  • Travel Time: 6 minutes
  • Hike Length: 1.1 miles
  • Elevation Change: 250 feet
  • Route Type: Loop

Community park with a partially shaded walking loop that borders Cartoogechaye Creek.

Greenway

All Greenway trails share these specifications: Travel Time 1 minute, Hike Length 1-5 miles, Elevation Change 190 feet, Route Type Out and Back.

13) Suli Marsh

Path through swamp land featuring an old metal bridge and boardwalks. Access off US-28.

14) Big Bear Park

Park on Main St. with playground, dog park, and trails providing access to old beaver dam area.

15) Riverwalk

Entrance behind The Shops at Riverwalk with a shelter, butterfly garden, and 0.25-mile exercise loop.

16) Salali Ln

Entrance point off Fox Ridge Rd with soccer fields, a pavilion, and swings.

17) Tassee Park

Features a pavilion and a boat put-in area off Ulco Dr. There is a unique geocache in this area.

18) Southwestern Community College

Trail connecting SCC to the greenway, alongside community garden plots near Old Siler Rd.

19) Library

Dirt road access down to the greenway and bottomland loop from the library on Siler Farm Rd.

Schools

These trails should be reserved for times outside of normal school hours.

20) Cartoogechaye Loop

  • Travel Time: 5 minutes
  • Hike Length: 0.25 miles
  • Elevation Change: 0 feet
  • Route Type: Loop

A partially shaded loop with a pavilion uphill from Gibson Cove Branch.

21) Cove Branch (MVI)

  • Travel Time: 4 minutes
  • Hike Length: 0.3 miles
  • Elevation Change: 0 feet
  • Route Type: Out and Back

A trail and restoration project at Macon Valley Intermediate School.

22) Macon Middle Loop

  • Travel Time: 4 minutes
  • Hike Length: 0.5 miles
  • Elevation Change: 20 feet
  • Route Type: Loop

A cross country trail circling the school's fields. Perfect area for flying kites.

23) South Macon Loop

  • Travel Time: 4 minutes
  • Hike Length: 0.5 miles
  • Elevation Change: 20 feet
  • Route Type: Loop

A paved loop with a spur trail that leads to a nice access on Skeenah Creek. Keep an eye out for birds, butterflies, and salamanders.
